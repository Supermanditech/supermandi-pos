SuperMandi POS

Your store's point of sale, right on your phone.

1

Open Expo Go on your Android device. Get it from Play Store if you haven't already.

2

Ask your SuperMandi admin for the enrollment code that was sent to your phone via SMS.

3

Open the POS app link shared by your admin, or scan the QR code to launch directly in Expo Go.

4

Enter your enrollment code to connect the device to your store. You're ready to sell.
