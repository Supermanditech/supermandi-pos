## Design

### POS (PurchaseScreen)
- Collapsible "Supplier Details (Optional)" section between item list and action bar
- Walk-in supplier name (free text) + GSTIN (15-char, auto-capitalize)
- Badge indicator shows "GSTIN" or "Name" when info entered
- Fields are optional — submit works with or without supplier info

### Backend
- GSTIN format validation: `^[0-9]{2}[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}[1-9A-Z]{1}Z[0-9A-Z]{1}$`
- Stored as `supplier_gstin` on `inventory.inventory_ledger` and `purchases`
- Partial index for analytics queries (WHERE supplier_gstin IS NOT NULL)

### SuperAdmin Analytics
- Stock-in breakdown by supplier type:
  - **Verified** — has `supplier_id` FK to supplier.suppliers
  - **Walk-in** — has notes/name but no FK
  - **Unknown** — neither
- Shows: Type, Supplier Name, GSTIN, Entry Count, Total Amount
- Row-spanning for type grouping
